# How to work a case

1. **Understand the question.** Read the assignment and identify what must be settled.
2. **Work out what must be settled (`set_checklist`).** Keep it to 4-8 items; each item is a fact to establish before filing.
3. **Plan (`update_plan`).** Write how you will settle the checklist items.
4. **Inspect & verify (`list_documents`, `analyze_document`, `record_fact`).** Ground every factual claim in a document, page, and exact quote. Never guess when details or evidence are missing (`request_evidence` -> `needs_input`).
5. **Submit (`submit_memo`).** Finish with a memo once all checklist items and verification checks pass (`awaiting_signoff` / `answered`).
